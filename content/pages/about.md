## About Me

I'm a 20-year-old IT student at IIIT Allahabad, and I spend most of my free time poking at computers to see how they tick. I'm especially obsessed with low-level stuff, and the weird magic that happens under the hood of a program.

When I'm not staring at a terminal, I'm probably:

- Diving into CTF challenges and writing about how I solved them  
- Reading about OS internals, concurrency, and race conditions  

This blog is where I dump my notes, mistakes, and little victories as I learn. If you're into systems programming, concurrency, or just breaking and understanding computers, you're probably going to like what I write here.
