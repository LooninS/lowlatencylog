I’m Sourish, a second-year IT student at IIIT Allahabad. I spend most of my time somewhere between systems, security, and low-level programming, basically anything that forces me to confront what the machine is actually doing instead of what I think it’s doing.

I like taking things apart. Sometimes that means stepping through binaries in a debugger, sometimes it means reading source code line by line, and sometimes it just means breaking something and sitting with the mess until it starts to make sense. Clean abstractions are useful, but they hide too much. The interesting parts are where they fail—where assumptions leak, where undefined behavior creeps in, where a small oversight turns into a vulnerability.

This blog is not a collection of polished tutorials. It’s closer to a working log. A place where I write things down while I’m still figuring them out. You’ll see incomplete thoughts, wrong turns, and the occasional moment where everything suddenly clicks after hours of confusion.

Most of what I write comes from things I’m actively working on:

+ CTF challenges (OverTheWire, PicoCTF, and whatever else I get stuck on)
+ Notes on binary exploitation, reversing, and system internals
+ Low-level concepts that take time to really internalize
+ Small, easy-to-miss details that end up mattering more than expected

I try to focus less on “here’s the solution” and more on “here’s how I got there.” The mistakes, the dead ends, the tiny observations—those tend to stick longer than clean answers.

I use Arch (btw), live mostly in the terminal, and prefer understanding tools over just using them. If something works, I want to know why. If it breaks, I want to know exactly where and how.

The main reason this exists is simple: writing forces clarity. If I can’t explain something (even badly) I probably don’t understand it yet. This is my way of fixing that, one post at a time.

If you’re into digging past the surface, questioning how things really work, or just watching someone else figure it out in real time, you’ll probably find something here.
