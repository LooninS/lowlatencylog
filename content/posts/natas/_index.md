This section is my running devlog for the **Natas** wargame.

I am solving each level one at a time and writing down:
- what the challenge gives me,
- what I notice first,
- the wrong paths I try,
- the key vulnerability or idea,
- and the final method that gets me through.

The goal is not just to record solutions, but to document the thought process. For security practice, that matters more than the final payload.

Most entries are short and practical. When a level introduces a useful recurring idea, such as client-side trust, weak access control, source disclosure, cookies, or request tampering, I try to make that pattern explicit so I can reuse it later.
